Small habits, useful progress

Career tips and insights

01

Choose one learning path for the next few weeks instead of collecting unrelated tutorials.

02

Turn every topic into a small project or explanation you can show and discuss.

03

Keep an honest record of what you built, what broke, and what you learned.

04

Practice explaining technical work in plain language for both technical and HR conversations.

05

Review your progress regularly and adjust your roadmap when your interests become clearer.
