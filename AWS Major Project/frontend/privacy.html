ShopEase Privacy Policy

Your privacy is non-negotiable. Learn how ShopEase collects, encrypts, and handles your data with strict zero-sale protocols and full compliance with DPDP Act and GDPR frameworks.

Last Updated: September 2026 • Zero Third-Party Ad Brokering • 256-Bit TLS In-Flight Encryption

1. Information We Collect

When you visit or place orders on ShopEase, we collect information strictly necessary to fulfill your purchases, safeguard your account, and provide real-time delivery telemetry:

  • Account & Contact Credentials: Your full name, delivery address, phone number, and email address when you register an account or complete checkout.
  • Order History & Fulfilment Details: Purchased hardware SKUs, quantities, payment authorization tokens, delivery instructions, and courier milestones.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and timestamp of order placement to prevent fraudulent bot transactions and account takeover attempts.

2. How We Use Collected Data

Your information is processed solely for legitimate commercial commerce operations:

  • Dispatching order confirmation notifications, GST invoices, and automated SMS/WhatsApp delivery tracking updates.
  • Coordinating doorstep pickup and replacements with our verified logistics carriers (BlueDart, Delhivery).
  • Authenticating manufacturer warranty registrations with certified brand OEM databases (Apple, Sony, Samsung, Dell, Bose).
  • Detecting payment fraud and maintaining AWS cloud infrastructure integrity.

3. Our Zero-Sale Commitment & Non-Disclosure Guarantee

ShopEase does not, has never, and will never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information with data brokers, ad networks, or external marketing entities.

We only share minimal necessary details with trusted operational processors under strict Data Processing Agreements:

  • Logistics Partners: Delivery name, address, and phone number provided strictly for physical delivery completion.
  • Statutory Authorities: Disclosures strictly mandated by Indian taxation laws (e-invoicing and GST portal reporting).

4. Payment Card Security & Financial Tokenization

ShopEase complies with Payment Card Industry Data Security Standards (PCI-DSS Level 1) and RBI tokenization regulations.

We never store raw credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or Net Banking credentials on our application servers. All electronic transactions are tokenized end-to-end through regulated banking payment gateways protected by 256-bit TLS encryption.

5. Cookies & Browser Local Storage Usage

ShopEase utilizes browser local storage and essential session cookies strictly for core UI functionality:

  • Theme Preference: Storing your choice of Light, Dark, or System mode.
  • Shopping Cart & Wishlist: Remembering items added to your bag so you don't lose them across browsing sessions.
  • Authentication Sessions: Secure, cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT) for authenticated users.

6. Your Data Subject Rights (DPDP & GDPR)

You maintain complete dominion over your data. At any time, you can:

  • Request Data Export: Access a portable copy of all personal and order records associated with your account.
  • Rectify Inaccuracies: Update your delivery addresses, phone number, or name directly inside My Account.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): Request the permanent deletion of your customer profile, subject to statutory tax invoice record-keeping rules.
