What is Covered Under Official Brand Warranty

ShopEase operates with a strict direct-from-brand policy. When you purchase on ShopEase, your device comes with the exact same warranty coverage as purchasing directly from Apple, Sony, Samsung, Dell, or Bose company flagship stores:

  • Internal Hardware Malfunction: Logic board, processors, RAM, solid-state storage, sensor arrays, or display controller failures.
  • Acoustic & Driver Failures: Distortion, silent channels, ANC microphone defects, or driver imbalances in headphones and monitors.
  • Battery Health Safeguards: Premature battery capacity degradation (>20% drop within manufacturer warranty period).
  • 100% Genuine OEM Replacement: All repairs utilize certified original factory components preserving dust/water resistance ratings and FCC/BIS certifications.
