Shipping Policy & Delivery Timelines

ShopEase partners exclusively with tier-1 national logistics providers including BlueDart Air Express, Delhivery Prime, and Shadowfax to guarantee rapid, insured, and GPS-tracked doorstep deliveries across all operational PIN codes in India.

Express Air Shipping
2 – 3 Business Days

Metro cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune).

Standard Surface Delivery
4 – 7 Business Days

Tier-2 and Tier-3 regional towns across all states and union territories.

All packages are dispatched within 24 business hours of payment verification from our centralized fulfilment centers in Bengaluru and Gurugram. Once handed over to the courier partner, an automated SMS and email containing the tracking URL is immediately dispatched.

Delivery Charges & Free Shipping Threshold

  • Orders above ₹1,999: 100% FREE Express Shipping across India. Zero hidden handling or packaging charges.
  • Standard Orders under ₹1,999: A nominal flat logistics fee of ₹99 is applied at checkout to cover insured courier handling.
  • High-Value Hardware: All laptops, smartphones, and professional monitors above ₹25,000 include complimentary tamper-proof security seals and OTP-verified delivery.

7-Day Hassle-Free Replacement & Returns Policy

We stand behind every authentic product we sell. If your order arrives defective, physically damaged, missing accessories, or differs from the catalog description, you may initiate a return or replacement within 7 days of confirmed delivery.

Eligibility Requirements:

  • Product must be accompanied by the original brand retail packaging, user manuals, warranty documentation, and bundled accessories.
  • Serial numbers and IMEI tags must match the records on your ShopEase order invoice.
  • Items must be free from customer-induced physical damage, liquid exposure, or unauthorized firmware modifications.

Refund Timelines & Payment Methods

Once our verification team receives and inspects the returned item at the fulfillment facility (typically within 48 hours of pickup), refunds are approved and processed immediately:

  • Credit / Debit Cards: 3 – 5 business days depending on your issuing bank.
  • UPI & Net Banking: Instant to 24 business hours.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Refunded directly to your bank account via NEFT/IMPS after providing account details in your dashboard.
